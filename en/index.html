FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I customize the checklist?

Yes. You can rename goals, add new tasks, choose weekdays and hide items you no longer need.

Does the tracker work for rotating shifts?

Yes. Choose the shift work routine to plan alternating weeks, record days off and correct individual dates.

Can I use the same account on my phone and computer?

The app is built around email accounts and Supabase-backed records. Sign in with the same account to access the same data. A working internet connection is required to save changes.

Are my records visible to other users?

Records are stored by account with database access rules. Use a strong password, do not share your login, and sign out on shared devices.
