# ABREDA editorial policy — effective 2026-11-25 KST

- No mass-produced template prose for blog posts, tool introductions, instructions, SEO copy, or FAQs. Shared design and structured data fields are allowed; substantive writing must be specific to the topic.
- Write original Korean and English copy for the intended reader; never copy another site or recycle another article's explanation. Do not publish shallow paraphrases or generic padding.
- Match search intent; use accurate titles, unique descriptions, descriptive headings, canonical and language alternates, and relevant internal links. Avoid keyword stuffing and misleading claims.
- Where explanation is needed, include concrete steps, verified inputs/outputs, examples, failure cases, alternatives, and limitations specific to the tool or problem. Do not invent functionality, testing, sources, or results.
- Check implementation and primary references before marking content reviewed. Verify both language versions, and check repeated paragraphs and semantic overlap before publication.
- For web apps, write original capability-specific help and link the matching article where it exists; preserve existing UI and actual functionality. For blog posts, use unique, relevant licensed photos and avoid changing the approved layout.
- Publication must be blocked when content is copied, generic, misleading, or unverified. Automated exact-duplicate checks supplement editorial review; they do not certify originality or catch all paraphrases.
