Fuel Cost Calculator

Calculate fuel use and travel cost using distance, actual fuel economy and the fuel price you enter. Supports kilometers per liter or miles per US gallon.

How to use the result

Use a consistent system: kilometers with km/L and price per liter, or miles with miles per US gallon and price per US gallon. Enter total round-trip distance if relevant. The calculation uses the fuel economy you provide and excludes parking, tolls, depreciation and changes in consumption from traffic or weather.

Your input is processed locally in the browser.
