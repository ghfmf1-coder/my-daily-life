Frequently asked questions

How is the dialogue ratio calculated?

It estimates the share of non-empty lines starting with opening quotation marks. Dialogue embedded in narration or formatted differently may not be counted correctly.

Can this proofread my grammar or spellcheck?

No. It highlights potentially repetitive words and unusually long passages so you can revise your draft yourself. It does not claim to be a full AI proofreader.

Does this tool train AI on my writing?

No. The page does not send the draft to an AI model or a backend service.
