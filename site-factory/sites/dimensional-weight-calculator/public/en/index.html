Dimensional Weight Calculator

Compare actual package weight with dimensional weight using box dimensions in centimeters and a carrier divisor you enter.

How to use the result

Measure the outermost dimensions after packing, including bulges or protrusions. Obtain the divisor for your exact carrier and service, expressed in cubic centimeters per kilogram. The tool shows the larger of actual and dimensional weight before carrier rounding, size surcharges or billing exceptions. It is not a shipping quote.
