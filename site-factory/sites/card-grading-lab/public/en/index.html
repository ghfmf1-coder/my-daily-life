How to measure

  1. Photograph the front or back of the card straight-on in even lighting.
  2. Upload the image and align the blue rectangle with the card's outer edges.
  3. Adjust the green rectangle to the inner printed design border.
  4. Review centering ratios and record any visible concerns.
  5. Measure the reverse side separately and compare both results.
Not affiliated with PSA or any grading company. The common PSA 10 centering reference (approximately 55:45 front, 75:25 back) is informational only. No grade, price, or grading outcome is guaranteed. Frameless designs may not be suitable for this measurement.
