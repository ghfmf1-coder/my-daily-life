Scanned PDF Compressor

Rasterize scanned PDF pages at a chosen resolution and JPEG quality, then compare the actual output size against the source.

This is a rasterizing workflow for image-based documents. Searchable text, OCR layers, hyperlinks, form fields, signatures and accessibility tags are lost. Never use it to preserve a signed or interactive document. The output may be larger than the source. Open the result and verify every page is readable before submitting it.
