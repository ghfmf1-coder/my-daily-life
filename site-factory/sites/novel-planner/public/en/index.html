Frequently asked questions

How do I find unresolved foreshadowing?

Add a plot thread and leave the payoff chapter empty. It remains in the unresolved list. If the payoff chapter comes before its introduction, the planner flags it for review.

Can I plan a serialized fantasy or romance novel?

Yes. This is a genre-neutral chapter outline and character tracker suitable for web fiction, serial novels, fantasy, romance, and more. You decide which beats and payoffs matter.

Are my story ideas uploaded?

No. Editing and storage happen locally in your browser; there is no account or server upload.
