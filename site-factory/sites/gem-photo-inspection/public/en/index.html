Generate a report from your own observations. Checked items are not AI detections.

Complete the fields and generate a report.

This report cannot determine whether a gemstone is natural, synthetic, treated, or authentic. Seek qualified laboratory testing when needed.

How to use

  1. Enter the gemstone description and measured dimensions.
  2. Optionally select close-up photos in consistent lighting.
  3. Record only defects or uncertainties you personally observe.
  4. Export the report for later independent verification.
Terms and disclaimer

This tool is for preliminary documentation only. Measurements, observations and reports do not guarantee authenticity, quality, value, safety or certification. Independently verify important decisions with a qualified gemologist. Nothing here excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded.
