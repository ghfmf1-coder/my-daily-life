Recipe Serving Calculator

Scale ingredient quantities from the original recipe yield to a new serving count, preserving the unit you enter for each ingredient.

How to use the result

Enter ingredients as lines such as flour,200,g. Both serving counts must be positive. The result scales numeric quantities and keeps the supplied units; it does not convert cups to grams. Whole eggs, salt, spices, pan capacity and cooking time may need judgment rather than linear scaling. Check food safety and doneness separately.
