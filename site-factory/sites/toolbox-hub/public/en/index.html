About these tools

Do I need an account?

Most editing and data tools run locally in your browser and do not require an account. The daily routine tracker uses an account so its records can sync across devices.

Are my files sent to your server?

The CSV merger and the manuscript analysis tool process the files and text in your browser. Do not share sensitive data in public forums when asking for support.
