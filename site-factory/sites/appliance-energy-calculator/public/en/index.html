Appliance Energy Calculator

Convert appliance watts and hours of use into daily and period energy consumption, with an optional cost calculation using your own electricity rate.

How to use the result

Enter the appliance rating and the number of hours it is actually operating. A thermostat-controlled heater may cycle on and off; a plug-in energy meter gives better evidence of real consumption. The cost uses a single rate you supply and does not model standing charges, taxes, time-of-use pricing or tiered utility tariffs.

Your input is processed locally in the browser.
