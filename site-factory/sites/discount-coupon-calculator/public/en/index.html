Discount and Coupon Calculator

Compare two checkout prices after a percentage discount, fixed coupon, shipping and an explicitly entered tax or other charge.

How to use the result

The calculation applies a percentage discount first, subtracts a fixed coupon without allowing a negative merchandise subtotal, and adds shipping and other charges. Enter any applicable tax in those charges. Stores may prohibit stacking discounts, set a maximum coupon value or calculate taxes differently. Verify the order and conditions on the actual checkout page before buying.
