Business Days Calculator

Count weekdays between two dates, excluding holidays you enter. Choose whether the first day counts; the last day is included.

How to use the result

The final date is included and Saturdays and Sundays are excluded. Enter the actual holiday dates for your jurisdiction or employer; the calculator does not assume a national holiday calendar. Duplicate holidays and holidays already falling on a weekend are not counted twice. Official filing deadlines, contractual notice periods and shift schedules may follow other rules.

Your input is processed locally in the browser.
