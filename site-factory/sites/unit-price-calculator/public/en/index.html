Unit Price Calculator

Compare grocery prices per 100 g, 100 mL, ounce or item. Enter the total package price and quantity to see which option costs less per unit.

How to use the result

Use the same currency and measurement unit for both packages. Add the net contents of multipacks before entering the quantity. To compare ounces, use ounces for both products and select the one-unit option. Weight and liquid volume are not interchangeable. A lower unit price does not account for spoiled leftovers, quality, or shipping charges unless you include those costs.
